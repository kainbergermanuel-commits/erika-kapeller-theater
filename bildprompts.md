# Bild-Prompts für die Requisiten

Für ChatGPT oder Gemini. Jeder Prompt ist vollständig, einfach so hineinkopieren.
Falls der Hintergrund nicht transparent kommt, im selben Chat nachschieben:
„Please export the same image as PNG with a truly transparent background, no checkerboard pattern.“

## 1. Maßband mit Schere (Stücke, „Nach Maß“)

```
Photorealistic product-style cut-out of a vintage cloth tailor's measuring tape in faded cream and gold, loosely curled around a small stack of three typewritten theatre script pages with a few handwritten pencil notes, a pair of antique brass tailor's scissors resting on top. Warm soft studio light from the upper left, gentle highlights on the brass, rich but natural colours, sharp focus, high detail. Isolated object on a fully transparent background, PNG with alpha channel, no shadow on the ground, no checkerboard pattern, no text or lettering, no logos. Seen from slightly above at a 30 degree angle. Square format, at least 1600 px.
```
